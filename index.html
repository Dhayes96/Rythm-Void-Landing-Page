Controls

How to play

Five things to know. You'll have them down inside one run.

  1. Tap when the ring reaches the target.
  2. Perfect hits restore energy and build combo.
  3. Misses drain energy.
  4. Hold the target or Spacebar to activate your equipped Boss Core ability.
  5. Survive as long as you can.

On mobile

Tap
Hit the target the moment the ring lands on it.
Hold
Press and hold the target to fire your equipped Boss Core ability.
Screen
Portrait works — turn your phone sideways for a wider view of the arena.

On desktop

Click
Click the target, or press Spacebar — both count as a tap.
Hold
Hold Spacebar or the mouse button to fire your Boss Core ability.
Audio
Headphones help. The rhythm is your best timing cue.
