How it plays

One tap. On the beat.

Rings close in on your craft in time with the music. Tap as each one lands. Perfect hits restore your energy, misses drain it, and the song only gets faster.

  • Phone · tap anywhere
  • Desktop · click, or press Space
  • Headphones · the rhythm is your best cue
