Privacy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Rhythm Void is a free browser game. This page explains, in plain terms, what the game stores and what it does not.

No account, no personal information

No account is required to play. No personal information is required to play. There is no sign-up, no login, and no profile.

What is stored on your device

The game may use your browser's localStorage to save your progress locally. That can include:

  • game progress, level and experience
  • settings and preferences, including audio and difficulty
  • scores, personal bests and leaderboard records
  • unlocks, including bosses and abilities
  • a player name, if you choose to set one

This data stays in your own browser on your own device. It is not sent to a server, and it is not shared with anyone. Clearing your browser data will delete it.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to collect basic anonymous usage information such as page views, device and browser type, and which buttons are clicked. This information is aggregated and is not used to identify individual players. It tells us which pages people reach and whether they find the game, nothing more.

Google may set cookies on your device as part of this. You can block them in your browser settings, or use a tracker-blocking extension, and the game will work exactly the same.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to playrhythmvoid@gmail.com.
